Traffic quality — monthly

What "non-engaged" means: GA4 marks a session "engaged" if it lasts 10+ seconds, has a conversion event, or has 2+ page views. A "non-engaged" session is someone who landed and left almost immediately — none of those three things happened. It's GA4's closest equivalent to classic "bounce rate," just inverted (non-engaged = bounced).
Sessions & avg session duration

August: traffic dropped to 478K sessions, avg session length nearly doubled (185s), and the non-engaged rate tripled to 13.6%, the highest of the period. Fewer people are visiting, and more of them are landing and leaving without engaging — worth checking if a site change or ad-mix shift happened around late July/August.

Detection algorithm

For CVR, Orders, Revenue, and AOV: take the trailing 30 days (excluding the day being checked), compute the mean and standard deviation. For each of the most recent 10 days, compute z = (value − mean) ÷ standard deviation. |z| ≥ 2.0 = High (statistically unusual — less than ~5% chance this is normal variation). |z| ≥ 1.2 = Medium (worth a glance). Below that = normal day-to-day noise, not shown.
